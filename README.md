# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Tableau** to explore Netflix content based on type, genre, rating, country, release year, and content trends.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
* Identify popular genres and content categories.
* Analyze Netflix content by country.
* Understand content growth over the years.
* Explore ratings and content characteristics.
